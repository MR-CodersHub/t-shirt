Quick Answers

Before You Reach Out —
Check the FAQ

How quickly will you respond to my message?
+
During business hours, we respond to all enquiries within 2 hours. Outside of business hours, you'll hear from us first thing the next business day. Bulk and event order enquiries are always given priority.
Can I visit your studio in person?
+
Yes! We welcome visits to our Brooklyn studio by appointment. Call or email ahead and we'll arrange a tour of the facility and a consultation with our design team. We're at 123 Print Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11201.
I have a custom project with unusual requirements — can you help?
+
Absolutely. We love challenging projects. Contact us with the details and our team will assess feasibility and provide a custom solution. We've handled everything from glow-in-dark festival merch to embroidered luxury brand jackets.
How do I track my existing order?
+
Log into your Customer Dashboard with your email and order number to view real-time production status and tracking information. You'll also receive automatic email updates at each stage.
My order arrived with a defect. What do I do?
+
We're sorry to hear that! Contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the defect. We'll arrange a free reprint and return shipping at no cost to you — no questions asked.
