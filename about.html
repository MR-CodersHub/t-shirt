Our Story

We Started With
One Big Dream

In 2018, three friends with a passion for streetwear culture and a single direct-to-garment printer started PRINTCRAFT in a Brooklyn garage. The dream was simple: make premium custom printing accessible to everyone — from indie creators to global brands.

Fast forward to 2026 — we've grown into a state-of-the-art facility with 50+ production staff, 12 print technologies, and over 2 million garments shipped to customers in 40+ countries. But our core values haven't changed: quality, creativity, and community.

Our DNA

What We Stand For

Three pillars guide everything we do — from the inks we choose to the way we answer the phone.

Uncompromising Quality

We'd rather lose money on a reprint than ship something that falls short of your expectations. Every order is quality-checked by human eyes before it leaves our facility.

Speed Without Sacrifice

Fast doesn't mean careless. We've engineered our production workflow to be both rapid and precise. 48-hour express is our badge of honor, not a compromise.

Community & Sustainability

We use water-based, eco-friendly inks and ethically-sourced blanks. We support local artist communities and donate 1% of profits to arts education programs.

Creative Freedom

Your design is sacred. We're here to execute your vision faithfully — and when you need inspiration, our creative team is always ready to help you find it.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees, no bait-and-switch. What you see in our quote is exactly what you pay. We believe honest pricing builds lasting relationships.

Customer First

24/7 support, dedicated account managers for bulk clients, and a rock-solid satisfaction guarantee. If something's wrong, we make it right — no questions asked.

Our Journey

Milestones That
Shaped Us

2018
The Garage Beginning
PRINTCRAFT launches out of a Brooklyn garage with one DTG printer and three founders who believe great printing shouldn't cost a fortune.
2019
First 10,000 Orders
We hit 10,000 orders and moved into our first real production facility. Added screen printing and embroidery capabilities.
2021
Online Design Studio Launch
We launched our web-based design studio, making it possible for anyone to create custom apparel from their laptop — no design experience needed.
2023
1 Million Garments Shipped
We celebrate shipping our 1 millionth garment and expand internationally. New facility opens in Austin, Texas.
2026
The Future is Bold
2M+ garments printed, 50+ team members, and the most advanced design-to-delivery platform in the industry. Just getting started.
