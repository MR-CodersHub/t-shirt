Pricing FAQ

Common Pricing
Questions

Are there any setup fees?
+
Zero setup fees on all DTG orders. Screen printing setup fees are waived for orders of 24+ pieces. Embroidery digitizing is included free with every order.
Do prices include the cost of the blank garment?
+
Yes! All our pricing is all-inclusive — it includes the blank garment, printing, and quality inspection. The only additional cost is shipping and any optional add-ons you choose.
Can I mix sizes in a bulk order?
+
Absolutely. You can mix any combination of sizes (XS–5XL) within a single order and still qualify for the full bulk discount based on your total piece count.
Do you offer payment plans for large orders?
+
Yes! Orders over $2,000 qualify for 50% deposit on order placement, balance due on shipping. Corporate accounts can apply for Net-30 invoicing terms.
