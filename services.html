FAQ

Common Questions
About Our Services

What file types do you accept for printing?
+
We accept AI, EPS, PDF, SVG, PNG (300+ DPI), and JPG (300+ DPI). For best results, vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) are strongly recommended. Our prepress team will review every file and contact you with any quality issues before production begins.
Can I get a sample before my full order?
+
Yes! We offer printed samples for a small fee. For orders over 100 pieces, samples are available free of charge. We strongly recommend a sample for bulk orders to ensure color accuracy and placement are exactly right before mass production.
How do I know which print method is best for my design?
+
Use our comparison table above, or chat with our design consultants. As a general guide: complex artwork → DTG; bulk bold designs → Screen Printing; logos on corporate wear → Embroidery; all-over patterns → Sublimation.
Do you offer Pantone color matching?
+
Yes! We offer Pantone Matching System (PMS) color matching for screen printing and embroidery at no additional charge. For DTG, we do our best to match your specified colors using CMYK calibration.
What is your return and reprint policy?
+
We offer free reprints on any order with manufacturing defects, print errors, or color deviations from the approved proof. Contact us within 30 days of delivery. We don't charge for our mistakes — period.
