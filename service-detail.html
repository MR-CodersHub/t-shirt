Service Overview

What is DTG Printing?

Direct-to-Garment (DTG) printing uses industrial inkjet technology to print your design directly onto fabric fibers. The result is a soft, breathable print with photographic detail and unlimited color reproduction — indistinguishable from the fabric itself.

Unlike screen printing, DTG requires no screens or setup — making it ideal for complex artwork, gradients, photos, and small run custom orders. Upload your file and we print it exactly as-is, at 600 DPI resolution.

The DTG Process

How DTG Printing Works

01
Pre-Treatment
The garment is pre-treated with a special solution that helps ink bond to the fabric fibers for optimal color vibrancy and wash durability.
02
Print Loading
The garment is loaded onto a flatbed platen and positioned precisely. Our equipment prints directly from your digital file at 600 DPI resolution.
03
Heat Curing
After printing, the garment goes through a heat tunnel to cure the ink — permanently bonding it to the fabric for lasting color and wash resistance.
04
QC & Ship
Every piece is inspected against your proof for color accuracy and placement. Then carefully packaged and shipped with live tracking.

DTG FAQ

Everything About DTG Printing

Is DTG print quality as good as screen printing?
+
DTG excels for complex, multi-color artwork and photographs. Screen printing has a slight edge for solid, bold colors at very high quantities. For most modern designs, DTG produces stunning results that match or exceed screen printing quality.
How should I care for my DTG printed garment?
+
Wash inside-out in cold water on a gentle cycle. Tumble dry on low heat. Avoid bleach and dry cleaning. With proper care, DTG prints last 50+ washes while maintaining vibrancy.
Can you DTG print on dark-colored shirts?
+
Yes! We use a white underbase for dark garments, which adds a slight additional cost (+$2/piece) but ensures colors are vivid on dark fabrics. Our results on black shirts are consistently excellent.
What's the minimum file resolution for DTG?
+
We recommend a minimum of 300 DPI at full print size. Our equipment prints at 600 DPI, so high-resolution files produce the most detailed results. Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) work perfectly at any size.
How long does DTG printing take?
+
Standard production is 2–4 business days. We offer 48-hour rush and same-day rush options for urgent orders. Exact timing depends on order size and current queue. You'll receive a confirmed timeline with your order confirmation.
