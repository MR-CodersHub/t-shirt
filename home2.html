FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our niche-specific printing services. Can't find your answer?

Talk to an Expert
What is the minimum order quantity for bulk printing?
+
We have NO minimum order requirement. You can order as few as 1 piece or as many as 100,000+. Bulk pricing tiers kick in at 12, 50, 100, and 500+ units.
How fast can you produce event shirts?
+
Our standard turnaround is 3–5 business days. We offer 48-hour and same-day rush options for urgent event needs. Rush fees apply based on quantity.
Can you print player names and numbers on jerseys?
+
Absolutely! We specialize in individual customization for sports teams. Upload your roster spreadsheet and we handle all the naming and numbering automatically.
Do you offer white-label services for clothing brands?
+
Yes! We offer full white-label and private label services including custom hang tags, inside labels, and branded packaging — perfect for streetwear and creator brands.
What file format should I submit for printing?
+
We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PNG (300+ DPI), and SVG files. Our design team will review every file and notify you of any quality issues before printing.
